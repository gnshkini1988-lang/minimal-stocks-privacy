Minimal Stocks - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23-11-2026

Minimal Stocks ("the app") is made by Ganesh Kini ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: there is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and I do not sell any data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your watchlists (the stocks and indices you add).
  • Your Scribble notes.
  • Your theme choice and cached market data.
  • If you choose to enter your own Finnhub API key, it is stored on your phone, encrypted with the Android Keystore where the phone supports it.

This information is not sent to me. It is removed when you uninstall the app. "Clear cache" in Settings removes the cached market data.

What the app sends over the internet

To show prices, charts and news, the app contacts these services. They receive the stock symbol or company name being looked up, and technical information that is sent with every internet request, such as your IP address.

  • Yahoo Finance: prices, charts, company information and search suggestions, including the text you type into search.
  • Google News: news headlines for Indian stocks.
  • My proxy server (hosted on Cloudflare): when you have not entered your own key, requests for US stock data go through a server I run, which forwards them to Finnhub and keeps short-lived copies of the answers. Cloudflare may keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, for a limited time.
  • Finnhub: market data. If you enter your own key, the app contacts Finnhub directly and sends your key to Finnhub only.
  • News publishers' image servers: the app loads article thumbnails from the websites that publish them.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and I do not control it.

Things you choose to do

  • Web search button: opens a Google search for the company name in an in-app browser tab. Google's privacy policy applies to that page.
  • Share chart: creates a picture in the app's temporary storage and hands it to the app you choose (WhatsApp, X, Reddit and so on). I do not receive it.

What I do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and I do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no sale of data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not investment advice

Prices, charts and news come from third parties and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. The app is for information only and is not investment advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date above and the new version will be on this page.
